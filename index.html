04 Projects

Engineered Solutions

01
AIR-1 National Award Winner

N-PHANTOM

Multifunctional Stealth Coating for Defence

An economical (21.6%) multi-signature coating system, applicable as a retrofit, that simultaneously reduces Radar Cross-Section (RCS), infrared emissions and thermal signatures. Integrates thermally stable acrylic/siloxane binders with 92% IR-reflective pigments and radar-absorbing ferrites for broadband EM attenuation, with corrosion resistance and field durability — supporting India's defence self-sufficiency.

02
CHEMATHON 2026 Finalist

Hydrothermal Reactor

Modular Single-Reactor Waste-to-Oil System

A modular HTL reactor converting unsorted municipal solid waste — food scraps, textiles, plastics — into liquid hydrocarbons, eliminating costly feedstock pre-sorting. Optimised temperature, pressure and residence-time control achieved 80–95% feedstock conversion and 70–85% liquid yield; recuperative heat exchangers recover 65–75% of process energy, making the system self-sustaining at pilot scale.
